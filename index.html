Problem and objectives

Executive summary

Manual and loosely coupled systems in multi-screen cinemas lead to double-booked seats at peak hours, overlapping schedules on one screen, inconsistent pricing, and data errors that surface only after payment.

These failures become revenue loss, unhappy customers and reconciliation work for staff.

Objective

Design a relational schema, normalized to Third Normal Form, where the database itself enforces the business rules.

Approach

Constraints, keys and transactions do the policing, so no application bug can create a double booking or a bad refund.

Rule 01 · Uniqueness

One seat,
one booking

A seat is assigned once per screening. If two customers click the same seat, the database accepts one and rejects the other.

UNIQUE (screening_id, seat_id)

Rule 02 · Scheduling

No overlapping
shows

Two screenings can never share a screen at the same time, and every show needs a 20 minute cleaning gap.

EXCLUDE USING gist
SHOW 118:00 – 20:10
SHOW 220:30 – 22:20
20 MIN
ERROR: conflicting key value violates exclusion constraint "screening_no_overlap"
COMMIT · Show 2 starts after the cleaning gap
03
Rule 03 · Money

Auditable
refunds

Cancellations follow the refund policy by hours before the show. The refund never exceeds what was paid, and every cancellation leaves an audit log.
